# Product brainstorming ideas

Ideas only, 2026-11-04. Nothing here is a decision, a price, or a build order. Each idea needs Ali's pick and a test with a real product before it moves into a prompt.

## Core product ideas
- Listing pack generator: images in order, title, description, 13 tags, pre publish checklist, Etsy ready JPG export.
- Reconciliation check: the tool compares tab count, price, format, and claims against the real product and stops a silent publish on a mismatch.
- Real product first: uploads are sheet PDFs, screenshots, or renders of the actual product. No AI photo pretending to be a real screenshot.
- Niche packs: start with wedding and events, personal finance, and small business planners. Test those three, keep other niches in backlog.
- Template sets per niche: hero, features grid, what is inside, how it works, mobile view, sizes and formats, and similar, 10 in v1, proved first with hero and features using the Maid of Honor Planner.

## Mockup ideas, see also mockup-ideas.md
- Device frames: laptop, phone, tablet, laptop plus phone hero, flat feature frame.
- Straight devices as default for Canva friendly use. Tilted phone as an option, with a clear warning about warping and white bands.
- Later frames to test: desk scene, printed book or planner, bundle of devices, seasonal background, social square crop.
- Seller custom frames: a seller can upload a frame later, after crisp rendering and no stretch rules are tested.

## Expansion ideas, Phase 2 or later, not v1
- Shopify and Amazon listing formats, built on the same reconciliation and export engine.
- Later platforms shortlist, added to the do list 2026-11-05, built later, in this order, rules to be verified from official sources before each is built: next after Amazon, Shopify, TikTok Shop, Facebook Marketplace and Instagram Shopping; then Walmart Marketplace, Google Shopping, Pinterest Shopping; then for Asia and Ali's audience, Shopee, Lazada, Daraz, plus WooCommerce as a store platform pack. Shopify and WooCommerce are own store product pages, TikTok Shop fits Ali's TikTok traffic, Facebook and Instagram are social commerce, Walmart and Google Shopping are large US marketplaces, Pinterest is vertical lifestyle discovery, Shopee, Lazada and Daraz are Asia and Pakistan region marketplaces.
- Video storyboard for a listing video, not video generation in v1.
- Multi language listings, gated by demand, same logic as Global Career Studio: English first, German, French, and Spanish only after demand is shown.
- Share and review link for a draft listing pack, so a partner can check it before export.
- Usage analytics that count use only, without reading product content.

## Ideas to test with real sellers, before building
- Do sellers want the tool to fix a mismatch, or only to flag it and let them fix the source product?
- Is a 7 day trial, no card, one per account, the right trial for a listing tool, or should the trial be per listing pack?
- Should mockups be free for all, or a premium feature, when the core listing pack is free to build and paid to export in full?
- Which niche pack actually converts first: wedding planners, finance planners, or small business planners?
