# Mockup brainstorming ideas

Ideas only, 2026-11-04. Added to the Listing Photos Generator idea at Ali's request.

## Principle
A mockup is a presentation frame. The screen inside it is always the seller's real product. A mockup never invents features, tab counts, prices, reviews, badges, or claims, and it is never labelled as a real screenshot.

## Starter mockups, v1 test set
- Laptop alone, straight on, for desktop products such as Google Sheets planners.
- Phone alone, straight on, for mobile view.
- Tablet alone, straight on.
- Laptop plus phone hero, the combination already proven in the Maid of Honor Planner blush set, with large screens covering most of the image.
- Flat feature frame, for a single sheet or feature close up.

## Mockups to brainstorm and test next
- Desk scene: laptop on a desk with a notebook and coffee, for a warm, lived in hero, only if the product screen stays crisp and readable.
- Printed planner or book: for sellers who also sell a printable version, clearly separated from a Sheets product, never mixed in one listing claim.
- Bundle of devices: laptop, tablet, and phone together, for a works on all devices story, only with real renders for each device, no invented device claim.
- Seasonal backgrounds: soft seasonal colour only, never a change to the product colours inside the screen.
- Social square crop: a square version of a hero mockup for social posts, exported separately from the Etsy landscape set, so Etsy sizes stay consistent.
- Before and after frame: a messy planning problem on one side and the organised sheet on the other, only with real sheet data, no invented before example.

## Fit problems to solve in the mockup engine
- Landscape sheet in a portrait phone: show a warning, offer a portrait screenshot or an approved crop, never stretch to fill.
- Wide sheet in a narrow phone: offer zoom out or horizontal scroll preview note, never pretend the sheet is phone optimised when it is not.
- Straight versus tilted: straight is the default for Canva friendly use. Tilted needs a warp step, like Photopea, and must be tested for white bands and blur.
- Orientation consistency: a listing set should not mix landscape and portrait frames without a reason, the Maid of Honor Template 08 portrait slip is the warning example.

## How new mockups get added, expandable by design
- Mockups live in admin, not hard coded: frame image, screen area, allowed orientations, background, and niche packs it suits.
- Add, duplicate, edit, enable, or hide a mockup with preview and versioning, the same rule as templates and copy in Global Career Studio.
- A new mockup is only added to the live library after a crisp render test: contain fit, centred, no stretch, no colour change, no crop unless approved.
