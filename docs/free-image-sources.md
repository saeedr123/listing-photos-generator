# Free legal image sources for Listing Photos Generator

Checked 2026-11-05 from official licence pages. This is not legal advice. Licences can change. Recheck each licence before launch, and recheck again before any new source is added.

## The problem to solve

Listing Photos Generator needs images in two different roles, and the licence rules are different for each role.

1. Background and lifestyle photos inside a listing image, for example a desk, a wedding table, a soft blush texture, or a celebration scene behind a template.
2. Device mockup frames, laptop, phone, tablet, with realistic perspective, shadows and depth, into which the seller's real product render is placed.

In both roles, the image must never be sold or given to the seller as an unaltered standalone file, and the tool must never become a stock photo library. That is the line every free licence below draws.

## Recommended solution

Use three layers, in this order.

### Layer 1, primary, build our own

Build premium device frames and backgrounds in house, at high resolution, and own them outright.

- Photograph or render our own laptop, phone and tablet scenes, with our own desk, lighting, shadows and background.
- Make our own plain backgrounds and textures, blush, ivory, soft paper, wood, linen, with no recognisable person, logo, brand or trademark in frame.
- This layer has no licence risk, no attribution, no model release question, and no risk that a licence changes later.
- Use Layer 1 for the hero mockups and for the 2 proof templates first. Free stock is a supplement, never the hero frame.

### Layer 2, free legal stock photos, transformed use only

Use Pexels first, Unsplash second, Pixabay third, only as transformed elements inside a finished listing image.

- A stock photo may be a background, a cropped detail, or a scene behind text and the seller's real product. It must be combined with other creative elements, so the finished listing image is a new work, not the stock photo on its own.
- Never offer a stock photo as a standalone download, never offer a stock photo library to browse inside the tool, never let a seller export the stock photo unaltered, and never put a stock photo on a stock or wallpaper platform.
- Record every stock image used: source, photographer, image page URL, licence name, download date, and what was changed. Store that record in the admin audit log. If a licence changes, the record shows what applied at download time.
- Credit the photographer where the licence asks for it in an app context, and where practical even when it is not required. Do not put a credit line inside a seller's Etsy listing image unless the seller approves it.

### Layer 3, free device mockup frames, per file licence check

Use only free mockup files whose own licence allows commercial and client work, and check the licence of each exact file before use.

- Allowed, verified in a comparison checked 2026-10-31: ls.graphics free section, 300 plus free 3D rendered mockups, allowed in personal and commercial projects including client work, not for resale, sign in required.
- Allowed: Mockups Design, licence allows any purpose including commercial, no attribution, you may not redistribute the source PSD files or resell them on stock sites.
- Allowed: Unblast own designs, free for personal and commercial use, no account needed to download.
- Not allowed on a free plan: Mockuuups Studio free exports, personal non commercial use only with visible attribution. Artboard Studio free users, personal licence only. Placeit and Mockey, commercial use tied to paid features.
- Mockup World is a directory, not a licence. Each mockup links to the creator's own site, so the creator's licence on that page is the one that counts.
- Never redistribute a mockup source PSD, never resell a mockup frame, and never use a mockup frame that still contains another seller's product, watermark, review count, badge or claim.

## Verified licences, 2026-11-05

### Pexels, primary free stock source

Source: https://www.pexels.com/license/, read 2026-11-05.

- Allowed uses listed on the official page include: use on your website, blog or app; use in an e-commerce shop, newsletter, e-book, presentation, blog or a template you sell; create unique ads, banners and marketing campaigns and promote your product; print marketing material; share on social media.
- That template you sell wording is the closest match to our use, which is why Pexels is first.
- From the Pexels Licence and Terms, as summarised in secondary sources checked the same day: all photos and videos are free to use, modify and use commercially, attribution is not required, but you cannot sell unaltered copies, you cannot imply that people pictured endorse your product, you cannot use images in a defamatory or offensive way, and you cannot redistribute Pexels content on competing stock platforms.
- Open item for Phase 2: one secondary source reported Pexels API new key issuance is paused. Do not rely on the Pexels API until Phase 2 checks the official API page and confirms whether a key can be obtained. Until then, use manual download with a recorded licence, not live API search.

### Unsplash, second free stock source

Source: https://unsplash.com/license, read by live browser 2026-11-05.

- Allowed: all images can be downloaded and used for free, for commercial and non-commercial purposes, no permission needed, attribution appreciated but not required.
- Longform licence, in substance: an irrevocable, nonexclusive, worldwide copyright licence to download, copy, modify, distribute, perform and use images for free, including for commercial purposes, without permission from or attributing the photographer or Unsplash.
- Prohibited: images cannot be sold without significant modification. Compiling images from Unsplash to replicate a similar or competing service is expressly excluded from the licence.
- Consequence for the tool: do not build an Unsplash browser inside the tool. Use only curated Unsplash images, transformed into a finished listing image, with the source recorded. A stock browsing feature would be compiling images to replicate a similar service.

### Pixabay, third free stock source

Source: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/, read by live browser 2026-11-05. The page states only the full Content License is legally binding, so Phase 2 must read the full licence before Pixabay is used in production.

- Allowed in the summary: use Content for free, use without attributing the author, modify or adapt Content into new works.
- Prohibited in the summary: you cannot sell or distribute Content, in digital or physical form, on a Standalone basis, where Standalone means no creative effort has been applied and it remains in substantially the same form as on Pixabay. You cannot use Content containing recognisable trademarks, logos or brands for commercial purposes in relation to goods and services, in particular you cannot print that Content on merchandise or other physical products for sale. You cannot use Content in an immoral or illegal way, especially Content which features recognisable people. You cannot use Content in a misleading or deceptive way. You cannot use any Content as part of a trademark, design mark, trade name, business name or service mark.
- The summary page does not contain a separate general statement that commercial use is allowed, and it does not name redistribution on other stock sites. Treat Pixabay as third choice, and only after the full licence is read in Phase 2.
- Pixabay also hosts AI generated content since April 2023, with unsettled copyright status. Prefer human made photos, and filter out AI generated content where the site offers that filter.

## People, brands and trademarks, guardrail for all sources

- Avoid images with recognisable people unless a model release is confirmed for that image. No free platform in this list guarantees a model release. Do not use a recognisable person in a way that implies they endorse a product.
- Avoid images with recognisable logos, brands, trademarks, or distinctive buildings, unless the use is clearly allowed. Do not use any stock image as a trademark, logo, business name or service mark.
- Avoid images that show a product, brand or person in a bad light, or in a misleading or deceptive way.
- Prefer images with no people, or with people shown only as hands, from behind, or out of focus, for listing backgrounds. That removes most model release risk while keeping a premium lifestyle feel.

## What is never a free legal source

- Adobe Stock watermarked previews, unlicensed PSDs, or any mockup lifted from Adobe Stock without a licence.
- Google Images, Pinterest, Instagram, Facebook, or any image found by search with no licence attached.
- Competitor listing images, including Hey Morning Studio and Productive Penguin Co sets. Those are structure reference only. Never use their sheet screenshots, reviews, badges, watermarks or branding.
- Any image that says Editorial Use Only, or that is marked as a sponsored partner tile on Pexels or Pixabay. Sponsored tiles lead to a paid site and are not covered by the free licence.

## Record keeping, to be built in Phase 2

- Every image asset in the tool, own built or free stock, must carry: source, photographer or creator, image page URL, licence name, download date, and a note of what was changed.
- Every mockup frame must carry: source, creator, licence, download date, and whether it is own built or licensed.
- Admin must be able to hide or replace an asset if its licence changes, without a code change and without breaking existing listing packs that already used it, except where the licence itself requires removal.
- No image asset is hard coded. All assets are admin managed, with preview, versioning and rollback, the same rule as templates and mockups.

## Open items for Phase 2

- Read the full Pixabay Content License, not only the summary, before Pixabay is used in production.
- Confirm whether a Pexels API key can be obtained, and what its rate limits and attribution rules are. If not, use manual download with recorded licences.
- Confirm the Unsplash API terms and rate limits, and confirm that curated transformed use, with no in tool stock browsing, stays inside the licence.
- Decide which own built frames to photograph or render first: laptop alone, phone alone, tablet alone, laptop plus phone hero, flat feature frame, in that order, at high resolution.
- Decide whether to add a CC0 only source, such as Openverse filtered to CC0, as a fourth source. That was not verified in this research, so it is not recommended yet.
