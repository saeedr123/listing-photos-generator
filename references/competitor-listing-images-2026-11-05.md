## What to mirror, structure only
- Premium layout: large serif title, small caps subtitle with bold key words, soft blush background with faint hearts and stars, white cards with soft shadow, rounded corners, clear section hierarchy
- Feature pages: one clear benefit title, one short benefit line, then a large real sheet preview, with small callout pills or badges at the top and bottom
- Device mockups: large laptop, tablet, and phone frames with realistic shadows and depth, screens covering most of the frame, not flat basic rectangles
- Closing pages: How it works in 3 steps, Why us in 3 cards, FAQ in 4 cards, Ready When You Are with Instant Download, Instruction, Support, plus a reviews panel
- Callouts that explain automation: Fully Automated badge, links to, colour coded results, progress rings and bars

## What NOT to copy, ever
- 20 Tabs, 16 Tabs, or any tab count that is not the seller's real tab count. Ali's Maid of Honor sheet has 12 tabs.
- 13,000 plus happy customers, 700 plus five star reviews, or any review text, names, dates, or star counts from these images. Those are another seller's reviews.
- Chartered Accountant, ADHD Friendly, Excel, Works On Any Device, or Choose Any Currency badges, unless the seller's real product actually has that property and it is verified.
- Hey Morning Studio watermark, Productive Penguin Co branding, logo, or promise text.
- Any sheet screenshot inside these images. Those are another seller's product, with its own data, figures, names, and dates. Ali's own sheet PDFs are the only sheet content to place in templates.
- Any price, currency, or device claim from these images, such as Microsoft 365 or Excel 2024, unless verified for the seller's real product.
